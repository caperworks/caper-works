Questions?

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need — a business website, a landing page, a store or a tool. Tell us about your project on WhatsApp and we'll send a clear quote before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

Landing pages are the quickest; stores and custom tools take longer. We agree on a timeline during Discover and keep you updated at every step.

Do you build on Shopify?

Yes. For D2C brands we build on Shopify or with custom code, depending on your products, budget and growth plans.

Will my landing page track ad results?

Yes. We set up Meta Pixel and Google Analytics with conversion events, so you can see which ads bring in leads and sales.

Is our data safe in a custom tool?

Each tool runs on your own subdomain, with a separate login and access level for every team member. The data belongs to you, and you can export it whenever you need.

Do you work with clients outside Jaipur?

Yes. We're based in Jaipur and work with clients anywhere over WhatsApp, calls and video meetings.

What happens after launch?

We offer maintenance and support to keep your site fast, secure and up to date — so it keeps working long after launch day.
