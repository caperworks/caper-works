Our Story

Over five decades of Jaipur craft

Vinayka Jewellery was founded by Shri L.N. Fatehpuria on one belief: jewellery is not merely an ornament but an heirloom, meant to pass from one generation to the next. What began as a small atelier in Johri Bazaar in the 1970s is now one of Jaipur's trusted names in fine jewellery, with a showroom on MI Road.

Signature Collection

Kundan & polki, the Jaipur way

Jaipur is known for its stones and its setters. Our signature pieces bring both together.

  • Hand-set stones

    Each stone is set individually for depth and fire.

  • Meenakari reverse

    Colour on the back, so the piece is beautiful from every side.

  • Built to be worn

    Balanced and comfortable, even through a long wedding day.
