Our Story

A Jaipur name, built one piece at a time

Vinayka began as a small workshop in Johri Bazaar and grew on one promise: honest gold, careful hands and jewellery you will still love in thirty years. Today our showroom on MI Road brings polki, kundan, gold and diamond work together under one roof.

The Craft

Gold, shaped by patient hands

Every piece passes through a karigar's hands before it reaches you. This is what you can expect from us.

  • Hallmarked purity

    Certified gold, marked and documented.

  • Handcrafted detail

    Setting, carving and finishing done by experienced artisans.

  • Made around you

    Custom designs, resizing and bridal orders handled start to finish.
Signature Collection

Kundan & polki, the Jaipur way

Jaipur is known for its stones and its setters. Our signature pieces bring both together.

  • Hand-set stones

    Each stone is set individually for depth and fire.

  • Meenakari reverse

    Colour on the back, so the piece is beautiful from every side.

  • Built to be worn

    Balanced and comfortable, even through a long wedding day.
