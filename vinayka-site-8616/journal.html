Guide

How to choose your bridal set

A bridal set is easiest to choose in the right order. Start with the outfit: the colour, the embroidery and the neckline decide which necklace will sit well.

Next, decide the hero piece. If the lehenga is heavily worked, a single bold necklace with simple earrings usually looks better than everything at once.

Finally, set the budget with weight and making charges in mind and ask for it in writing. A good jeweller will happily walk you through the numbers.

Bring the outfit

Even a photo or a fabric swatch helps us match tone and scale.

Try before you decide

Wear the pieces. Comfort over six hours matters more than it seems.

Care

Caring for kundan and polki

Kundan and polki are durable, but the settings and the meenakari reverse like gentle handling. Put your jewellery on last, after perfume and make-up, and take it off first.

Store each piece in a soft pouch, away from others that can scratch. Keep silica sachets in the box to fight moisture, especially in the monsoon.

Clean lightly

A soft dry cloth after wearing is enough. Avoid water and chemical cleaners on set stones.

Service yearly

Bring heavier pieces in once a year so we can check settings and clasps.

Style

Festive jewellery, simply done

The easiest festive rule: one statement, everything else quiet. If you wear a bold necklace, keep earrings small. If the earrings are the story, choose a fine chain.

Metals should agree with the outfit. Warm gold flatters rich colours, while diamonds and light stones suit pastels. Mixing is fine when one element clearly leads.

Start with the neckline

Round and high necks love chokers. V-necks suit longer pendants.

Edit at the mirror

Before leaving, remove one thing. It nearly always looks better.
