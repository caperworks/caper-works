Our Story

From a Johri Bazaar workshop to MI Road

Vinayka Jewellery was founded by Shri L.N. Fatehpuria with a singular belief: that jewellery is not merely an ornament but an heirloom, something meant to be passed from one generation to the next.

Our home, MI Road, sits in the city that has been the soul of Indian jewellery-making for centuries. Jaipur's master craftsmen, its gem markets and its tradition of kundan and meenakari work are our inheritance, and we carry that legacy in every piece we create.
